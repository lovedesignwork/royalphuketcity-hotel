A good rooftop dinner starts with a simple plan: choose the occasion, confirm a reservation for your date and group, and leave enough flexibility for the journey and the weather. In Phuket Town, TWIST Rooftop Restaurant & Bar is on the 19th floor of Royal Phuket City Hotel. Its city setting makes it an option for an evening after exploring town or a dinner connected to your hotel stay.

Rather than trying to fit every possible activity into one evening, decide what matters most. A sunset-focused visit, an unhurried meal and a small celebration each need slightly different arrangements.

What kind of evening do you want?

Start with the people at the table. Two guests marking an anniversary may want an easy conversation and a relaxed pace. Friends meeting after sightseeing may prefer a clear arrival point and a menu everyone has had time to consider. A family meal may need a different time and seating arrangement.

Choose one main priority and explain it when you enquire. “We would like a relaxed dinner for four” gives the restaurant a useful starting point. It is more practical than assuming a particular photograph represents the table, conditions or service available on your date.

What should you ask when reserving a table?

Use the TWIST venue page to find the restaurant and current enquiry options. Give your date, preferred time, party size and any details that affect the booking. If you have flexibility, include an alternative time so the team can respond with a useful option.

A request and a confirmation are different steps. Ask which parts of the arrangement have been accepted, whether payment is required and what happens if your plans change. Keep the confirmation where you can find it on arrival. Do not assume a room reservation also reserves a dining table.

How can you plan for daylight and changing weather?

If seeing the city before dark is important, say so when you enquire and ask about a suitable arrival time for your date. Sunset changes through the year, and cloud cover can change the experience. Avoid building an important occasion around a guaranteed colour in the sky.

Ask about the seating arrangement and alternatives if weather affects the plan. A specific table position should be treated as a request until the venue confirms it. Let the meal, company and pace of the evening carry the occasion, with the view as part of the experience.

Leave room between a town outing and dinner. Returning to change, meeting other guests and arranging transport can take more attention than a tightly packed itinerary allows.

How do you choose a meal everyone can enjoy?

Review the current menu through the venue page rather than relying on an old blog photograph or a remembered offer. Ask whether your party will order individually or whether a proposed group arrangement is available. Check the full cost of any agreed arrangement, including the charges the restaurant identifies.

Tell the restaurant about meal preferences and any essential restrictions before arrival. Ask what it can accommodate and get a clear response; do not assume that a dish can be changed because a similar one appears elsewhere. For a group, one organiser can collect the requests so the venue receives a coherent message.

Our hotel dining overview also introduces the other dining options at Royal Phuket City. Choose according to the meal your group wants, rather than expecting every venue to offer the same experience.

What changes when you are celebrating?

For a birthday, anniversary or reunion, mention the occasion early and describe the request clearly. Ask whether flowers, a dessert arrangement or another detail can be organised, who supplies it and whether there is a separate charge. These are enquiries, not inclusions promised by this guide.

Keep the plan manageable. One confirmed detail can feel more thoughtful than several uncertain extras. If something is a surprise, agree on the contact person and the point in the evening when it should happen. For a larger gathering, the hotel may need a separate event discussion rather than an ordinary table reservation.

How should you coordinate arrival and the journey home?

TWIST is inside Royal Phuket City Hotel at 154 Phang-Nga Road. Share the hotel address with everyone in the party, agree on a meeting point and confirm how to reach the restaurant on arrival. The location is in Phuket Town, so a journey from a beach-area hotel needs its own plan.

Guests planning a stay can compare the hotel's rooms and suites. Room availability, dining availability and any requested extras should each be confirmed for the relevant date.

A useful message to send with your enquiry

Keep the enquiry short but specific. You can adapt this structure when contacting the hotel:

We would like to enquire about dinner at TWIST on [your date] for [your party size], ideally around [your preferred time]. The occasion is [your occasion, if relevant]. Please confirm available times, seating options, the current menu and any booking or payment conditions. We would also like to ask about [your specific request].

Send personal details only through the venue's enquiry channel. Use our contact page to reach the hotel; do not treat a draft message as a completed reservation.
