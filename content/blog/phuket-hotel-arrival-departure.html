A morning landing and an evening flight home can leave two awkward gaps in a Phuket holiday: the hours before your room is ready and the hours after you have checked out. Plan those gaps before filling the rest of your itinerary. The useful question is not simply whether a hotel offers early check-in or late check-out, but what access has actually been confirmed for your booking.

Royal Phuket City Hotel is a city base on Phang-Nga Road in Phuket Town. This guide helps you organise your first and last day around your own travel arrangements, with room access, rest and departure plans kept separate.

Work with two clocks: travel time and room access

Write down your flight date and time, then the date and approximate time you expect to reach the hotel. These are different events. Allow for the stages between landing and arriving at reception, and confirm transport details with your chosen provider rather than building your plans around a fixed journey estimate.

Next, check the hotel dates on your reservation and the access arrangements that apply to it. A flight arriving in the early hours can be particularly confusing: reaching the hotel after midnight does not automatically mean the room booked for later that day is available immediately. Tell the hotel your expected arrival in a full date-and-time format, including the time zone, instead of saying only “Friday night”.

Ask about early check-in before relying on it

The hotel's published policy states that early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and may incur additional charges. Treat a request as pending until the hotel confirms the arrangement. A confirmed room reservation and a confirmed earlier access time answer different questions.

If being able to rest straight away is essential, explain that priority before booking. Ask which booking arrangement could provide access at the time you need, which dates it would cover and what the total charge would be. If a room for the preceding night is proposed, confirm the reservation dates and arrival instructions carefully. Do not assume an extra booking, day-use room or particular rate is available.

Make a flexible plan for the waiting period

Keep the first few hours easy to rearrange. A tightly timed activity immediately after arrival becomes difficult if travel takes longer or the room is not yet available. Choose a simple first-day plan that can move with your arrival rather than one that depends on every stage running to schedule.

Ask the hotel whether luggage can be held, when it can be collected and what arrangements apply. If you would like somewhere to sit, a meal or access to a facility while waiting, ask about each item separately. A room reservation should not be treated as confirmation that every waiting option is included before check-in.

Keep the things you may need during this gap accessible: your reservation details, travel documents, a charger and any personal essentials. Leave space in the plan to change direction once reception has confirmed your room's status.

Match the plan to the people travelling

A couple happy to explore and a family needing a quiet break may want different first days. Discuss this before choosing the room and arrival arrangement. Our family room and suite guide explains how layout and the travelling party affect a room enquiry; the arrival plan adds the question of when you need to use that space.

For people arriving separately, say who is expected first and ask how check-in should be handled. Give the hotel the relevant booking information through its official contact channel, without placing documents or personal details in a public group message. If plans change, update the hotel as well as the person arranging your transport.

Decide what you need before a late flight

Start with your flight and transport arrangements, then decide when you need to leave the hotel. Obtain your departure advice from the airline and transport provider, and leave an appropriate margin for your circumstances. Work backwards from that departure point to choose a meal, luggage collection time and any activities.

If you want to continue using the room, request late check-out and ask when availability can be confirmed, the latest agreed access time and any extra charge. Keep an alternative plan until you have that answer. If late access cannot be confirmed, ask about luggage arrangements and consider a separately booked meal or a flexible outing. Confirm whether any facilities you hope to use remain available after checkout.

Send one clear message before travelling

Include your reservation reference, hotel stay dates, expected arrival date and time, departure plans, number of guests and the access you are requesting. Ask the hotel to distinguish confirmed arrangements from requests still subject to availability. Recheck any charges, collection instructions and the contact to use if travel changes.
